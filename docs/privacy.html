VEZU Oversize Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

VEZU Oversize ("VEZU", "the app") turns oversize/overweight permit PDFs into a short driving brief and route. This policy explains what data the app handles, where it goes, and how long it is kept.

Summary

  • No account, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.
  • Your permits are saved only on your phone.
  • The permit PDFs you add are sent to the VEZU server and read by Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic. The app asks for your agreement before the first PDF is sent.
  • VEZU does not store your PDFs on its server. Anthropic deletes them within 30 days and does not use them to train its models.

Data the app sends

When you import a permit, or open a permit's route for the first time, the app sends the permit's PDF files to the VEZU server. A permit can contain personal information, such as the carrier's or driver's name, phone numbers, vehicle and trailer details (unit number, plate, VIN), load details, and the permitted route.

To place the route on a map, the app also sends road names, junctions, and place names from the route to the VEZU server and to Apple's map search. These requests contain no personal information.

The app does not access your location, contacts, photos, or any other data on your phone. It only reads the PDFs you choose to add.

How the data is used

The data is used only to write your permit brief, route, and translation. It is not sold, shared for advertising, or used to build a profile of you.

Who processes the data

  • VEZU server, hosted on Cloudflare Workers. It passes the PDFs to Anthropic and returns the result. It does not save the PDFs. Short diagnostic logs (error codes, token counts, and, when two readings of a permit disagree, short quotes from the permit's restrictions) are kept by Cloudflare for a few days and then deleted automatically. See the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.
  • Anthropic, which makes Claude. Anthropic deletes API inputs and outputs within 30 days and does not train its models on them, except where content is flagged for violating its Usage Policy or the law requires longer retention. See the Anthropic Privacy Policy.
  • Apple, for map search of route place names. See the Apple Privacy Policy.
  • Google Maps, only when you tap Navigate. The route's stop coordinates open in the Google Maps app or website, under the Google Privacy Policy.

Data stored on your phone

Your permits, their PDFs, briefs, and routes are saved only on your phone. Expired permits are deleted automatically 30 days after they expire. You can delete any permit at any time from the brief's ⋯ menu. Deleting the app deletes all of its data.

Your choices

If you don't agree to have your permits read by AI, tap Not Now. Nothing is sent, and you can still open the demo permit. Because VEZU keeps no copy of your data and has no accounts, there is nothing on our side to access or delete; Anthropic deletes its copy within 30 days.

Children

VEZU is a tool for commercial drivers and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
