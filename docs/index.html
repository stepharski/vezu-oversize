VEZU Oversize Support

VEZU turns oversize/overweight permit PDFs into a clear driving brief and sends the permitted route to Google Maps.

Common questions

How do I add a permit? Tap Import on the Permits screen and pick the PDF, or open the PDF in Mail, Telegram, or Files, tap Share, then tap VEZU.

The brief looks wrong. Briefs are written by AI and can contain mistakes. Always check the brief against your paper permit. You can tap Reload Permit in the brief's ⋯ menu to read it again.

Is VEZU truck navigation? No. Google Maps doesn't know your load size and may reroute off the permit. Always drive the permitted roads.
